Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: October 29, 2026

Privacy in To Software casual and arcade games.

This policy applies to To Software games that link to this page. Features, services and data handling vary by game and version. Use the same contact to ask questions or request deletion, identifying the game concerned.

1. Responsible party and covered games

To Software is responsible for the game data under its control. Our public support and privacy contact is tosoftwarestudio@gmail.com.

This policy covers Rainbow Jump, Color Collision and other To Software casual or arcade games that link to it. Naming a game here does not mean it has been released or that every feature below is active.

Rainbow Jump may offer local progress, optional Google Play Games, a Unity guest profile, rankings and rewarded ads. Color Collision keeps local progress and preferences; online and advertising features depend on the configuration and version made available. For other games, only processing corresponding to the features and SDKs in the version you use applies.

2. Data involved

Local progress and preferences: best score, completed levels, language, sound/vibration options and pending score synchronization records may be stored by the app. Clearing local data does not automatically erase online records.

Profiles and rankings, when offered: player ID, public name, score and session/authentication information are used by Unity Authentication and Unity Leaderboards. Public names and scores may be visible to other players. Google Play Games is optional; a game may automatically create or restore a guest profile without asking for your email or password.

To Software does not receive your Google password. The integration uses the platform authorization mechanisms. Linking a guest or recovering an existing Google profile does not automatically merge every account. An unlinked guest may lose access if the saved session is removed.

Advertising and technical data: active SDKs may process IP address, approximate location inferred from IP, advertising and other device/app IDs, device model, operating system, language, connection, app version, diagnostics and ad interactions. Some SDKs, including versions of Unity Ads, also disclose purchase-history processing for advertising and measurement; this does not mean every game offers purchases. The actual data depends on the SDK, version, feature and applicable choices. IP-based location is distinct from GPS access.

Support: when you contact us, we receive your email address and the information you send to handle your request. Do not send passwords, tokens or authentication codes.

3. Purposes

Applicable data is used to operate the game, save preferences/progress, authenticate profiles, synchronize scores and provide rankings; offer optional ads and grant rewards; measure service and advertising operation; diagnose failures and prevent fraud/abuse; and handle rights and legal obligations.

Depending on the purpose and applicable law, processing may rely on the requested service, legal obligations, legitimate interests assessed against your rights, or consent where required. Publishing this policy does not replace required consent.

4. Advertising and privacy choices

Where rewarded ads are offered, you choose to watch an ad for the stated reward, such as a revive opportunity. Unity LevelPlay may mediate delivery. Online services and ads require a connection and may be unavailable; local gameplay does not require watching an ad.

Choosing to watch an ad and consenting to personalized advertising are separate decisions. Consent, restrictions and privacy options depend on the region, SDK and applicable configuration. Use the privacy controls presented by the game or consent manager when available or required. A 13+ audience label is not proof that a player is an adult.

Android advertising and advertising-ID settings provide additional choices. These do not guarantee removal of every ad or all technical data necessary for the services.

5. Providers and sharing

Only providers included in a game version take part in the corresponding processing. These games may use:
  • Google Play Games, Google AdMob and Google UMP: platform/sign-in, advertising and privacy choices, as integrated. Google Privacy Policy.
  • Unity Authentication, Unity Leaderboards, Unity LevelPlay, Unity Ads and ironSource Ads: profiles, rankings, mediation and advertising, as integrated. Unity privacy policy for players.

Data may be received directly by SDKs or shared to operate, measure and protect the services and advertising, according to their purposes and policies. Disclosure may also be necessary for legal obligations, protecting rights or investigating fraud within applicable limits. Support email addresses are not published in rankings.

New services or material collection changes require updated disclosures for the game. A shared policy does not mean every game processes identical data.

6. Retention and deletion

Local data remains on the device while retained by the app or platform backups. Android storage controls can remove it. Uninstalling does not necessarily erase backups or data already sent to online services.

Online profiles and scores are retained while needed for the stated features and purposes, until requested deletion or another applicable procedure. Support messages are kept as needed to handle and document the request. Additional retention for legal duties, disputes or fraud prevention must be limited to the necessary purpose and period; relevant exceptions will be explained.

To request deletion of your profile and associated data under our control, see Account and data deletion. Identify the game or games concerned. This does not delete your Google account. Provider-held data is also subject to each provider’s procedures and retention criteria.

7. Your rights

Under applicable law, you may request information, access, correction, deletion and other available measures, and withdraw consent where it is the basis for processing. Withdrawal may affect features that depend on that consent.

Contact us with the game name. We may need minimal information to locate the profile and verify the request. Do not send credentials or unsolicited identity documents.

8. Security and international processing

We use measures to protect data under our control and the security mechanisms of integrated services, including encrypted connections for data sent to online services. No system eliminates all risk.

Providers may process information in other countries according to their infrastructure and the protections required by applicable law. Their policies provide further details.

9. Audience aged 13 and over

Covered games are intended for people aged 13 and over and are not directed to children under 13. This audience classification does not replace privacy and consent requirements that apply to teenagers.

You do not need to complete an age questionnaire on this website. If you are a parent or guardian and believe data was handled inappropriately, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

10. This website

These pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. We do not include ads, registration forms or our own audience-analytics tools here. The host may receive IP address, browser information and technical logs to deliver and protect the website. See the GitHub privacy statement.

Following external links or sending email also involves the services and policies of the respective providers.

11. Updates and contact

We update this policy when games, services or applicable requirements change. The date above identifies the current version. Changes will be communicated and new permissions requested where required.
