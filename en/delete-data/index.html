Account and data deletion

Effective and last updated: October 29, 2026

Request deletion of your profile and associated data for one or more To Software games. Guest profiles can also be included.

Request deletion by email

How to request deletion

Email tosoftwarestudio@gmail.com with the subject To Software — Data deletion — game name.

Identify which game or games are involved, your public profile/ranking name and, if available, the player ID for that game. If you do not know the ID or have lost a guest session, explain this; we will guide you through available options to locate and verify the profile. You do not need a Google account or to reinstall the game to start a request.

We may ask only for information necessary to verify ownership. Do not send a Google password, tokens, access codes or unsolicited documents. A public name alone may not be sufficient to verify ownership.

What can be deleted

After locating and verifying the profile, the request covers the game account/profile and associated data under our control, including public name, score/ranking records and other linked data where present. Services and data differ between games and versions.

Explicitly identify requests covering several games: deleting one game profile does not automatically delete the others or your Google account. Local data can be removed with Android storage controls; this does not replace online deletion. Signing in again after deletion may create a new profile.

Where legal duties, fraud prevention or protection of rights require retention, we will limit it to the necessary purpose and period and explain relevant exceptions. For provider-controlled data, we may guide or forward requests as applicable under their procedures.

Progress and contact

This is an email request channel, not automatic erasure. We will reply through the contact used with verification steps, progress, completion and applicable timeframes. If a guest profile cannot be identified or ownership verified, we will explain the available options and limitations.

If no email app is configured, copy the address and send the message through your email service. See the Privacy Policy for other rights and processing details.
